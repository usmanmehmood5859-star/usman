## Before bidding: requirements to confirm
Outside Alberta, Throne will need extra-provincial registration and WCB coverage in each province (WorkSafeBC, WCB SK, WSIB, WCB NS). Alberta owners and GCs commonly ask for a COR. Mount Royal University's 2026 program accepted **ARCA-certified** roofers only. BC public specs often require RCABC/RoofStar. Federal bids need a PBN, and some sites need security screening. None of these were verified for Throne; see the **Provincial Requirements** sheet.
